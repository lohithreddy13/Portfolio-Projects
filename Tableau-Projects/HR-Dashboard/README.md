
Human Resource Dashboard

An interactive Tableau HR analytics dashboard designed to help HR managers monitor workforce trends, understand employee demographics, and analyze compensation patterns through a combination of summary views and detailed employee-level insights. The published dashboard includes Overview, Demographics, and Income sections, along with interactive filters for gender, state, status, and hire date.

Project Overview

This project was built to transform HR data into a decision-support dashboard for business users. It provides a high-level snapshot of hiring and termination activity while also enabling deeper analysis of workforce composition, education, performance, salary patterns, and employee distribution across locations.

Dashboard Objectives

The dashboard is designed to answer key HR questions such as:

How many employees were hired, are currently active, or have been terminated?

How have hiring and termination trends changed over the years?

Which departments and job titles make up the workforce?

How is the workforce distributed across headquarters and branches, cities, and states?

What does the workforce look like by gender, age group, and education level?

How does education relate to performance ratings?

Are there visible salary differences across education levels, gender, age, and departments?

Dashboard Structure

1. Overview
The Overview section presents the core workforce summary and organizational distribution. It includes total hired employees, active employees, terminated employees, hiring and termination trends over time, department-level breakdowns, and the split between HQ and branches, with New York shown as the headquarters location.

2. Demographics
The Demographics section focuses on workforce composition. It shows the gender ratio, employee distribution across age groups and education levels, total employees within each age band, and the relationship between education level and performance rating.

3. Income Analysis
The Income section explores compensation patterns in the workforce. It compares average salary across education levels for both genders and visualizes how age and salary relate across departments.

Key Features

Interactive filtering by gender, state, status, and hire date.

Multi-section layout covering workforce overview, demographics, and income analysis.

Trend analysis for hiring and termination over time.

Department, job title, location, and education-based breakdowns.

Salary analysis by education level, gender, age, and department.

Geographic workforce distribution by city and state.

Tools Used

Tableau for dashboard development and interactive visual design.

HR dataset containing employee status, demographics, education, salary, department, location, and date fields inferred from the dashboard structure and filters.

Business Value

This dashboard helps HR stakeholders move from raw employee data to actionable insights. It can support workforce planning, attrition monitoring, diversity analysis, organizational review, and compensation pattern analysis through a single interactive reporting layer.
